## Algebraic Connections

### VP vs VNP

**Statement:** Is VP = VNP (algebraic classes over fields)?

**Relationship:**
- Algebraic analog of P vs NP
- VP ≠ VNP is often considered "easier" to prove
- GCT targets this problem

**Status:** Open.

### Permanent vs Determinant

**Statement:** Is the permanent as easy as the determinant?

**Relationship:**
- Permanent is #P-complete
- Reducing to determinant size would imply VP = VNP (in some sense)

**Status:** Open. GCT framework addresses this.
