## Family generators

C12 adds deterministic parameterized families:

- `or_n`
- `and_n`
- `reducible_n`
- `random_k_cnf` with deterministic seed-controlled pseudo-random literals

The positive-size family constructors are normalized so they do not contain
terminal neutral constants such as `∨ false` or `∧ true`. This prevents a
zero-restriction sample from receiving artificial payoff solely from syntax
cleanup.

`family,n,k,seed,width,restriction,alpha,beta,varsBefore,varsAfter,deltaVars,muBefore,muAfter,deltaMu,rankBefore,rankAfter,deltaRank,costBits,branchingCost,potentialBefore,potentialAfter,payoffLhs,paysOff`.

- `BranchingCost(ρ,t) = 2 ^ costBits(ρ,t)`
- `Potential α β t = α * vars t + β * stateRank t`
- payoff is the exact natural-number comparison
  `Potential α β (D ρ t) + costBits ρ t < Potential α β t`.
