# Horizon Prime

Status: RESEARCH_ONLY / SEMANTICS_NOT_YET_PROMOTED

Horizon Prime is the evidence lane for boundary phenomena in prime-state
structures discovered while FINAL_PROOF runs.

## Collect

- smallest/largest admissible prime state under a fixed denominator or constraint;
- central gaps after excluding 0, 1, 2, or other designated states;
- changes in gap width as denominator/numerator constraints vary;
- parity boundaries and shell transitions;
- first counterexample to any proposed monotonic or closure law;
- asymptotic candidate laws for gap/shell growth;
- invariants preserved by compression/reflection/expansion;
- points where a proposed Prime2 interpretation ceases to reflect the standard model;
- any relation between a horizon observation and encoding size, runtime, or semantic failure;
- exact negative evidence showing that a proposed horizon law does not generalize.

## Required distinction

A finite numerical pattern is evidence only. It does not imply an asymptotic
theorem, a complexity lower bound, G21Pointwise, or P != NP.

## Promotion target

A Horizon Prime observation becomes relevant to FINAL_PROOF only if a
kernel-checked theorem maps it into one of:

- ConservativePrime2Interpretation,
- MappedPrime2UniversalFailure,
- G21Pointwise,
- a real P01 physical/complexity obligation.

Until then: proof_credit = 0.
